Vaccination Programs

Step by Step

How We Rescue Every Animal

From the moment we get a call to the day a pet finds their forever home — here's our journey together.

1

Emergency Call

We receive rescue reports from community members and respond within hours to injured or abandoned animals.

2

Rescue & Transport

Our volunteers safely rescue the animal and transport it to our shelter with care and urgency.

3

Medical Treatment

Immediate vet assessment, treatment, surgeries if needed, and round-the-clock monitoring until recovery.

4

Forever Home

Once healthy, we match each animal with the perfect loving family through our adoption program.

More Programs

All the Ways We Care

Beyond our core programs, we run several specialized services to ensure complete wellbeing for every animal.

🏠

Shelter & Housing

Safe, clean, and loving temporary shelter for rescued animals while they wait for their forever home.

✂️

Spay & Neuter

Free sterilization programs to responsibly control the street animal population and improve their health.

🐾

Rehabilitation

Behavioral and physical rehabilitation for traumatized animals to help them trust humans again and thrive.

📋

Adoption Counseling

We guide every adopter with personalized advice on care, diet, training, and long-term pet parenting.

🎓

Community Awareness

School programs and community events educating people about animal welfare, empathy, and co-existence.

📞

24/7 Helpline

Round-the-clock rescue reporting line — because emergencies don't follow business hours, and neither do we.
