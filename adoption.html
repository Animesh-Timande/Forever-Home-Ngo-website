What Is Adoption?

More Than a Pet —
A Lifelong Bond

"Adopting means choosing a life that was once overlooked — and promising it will never be forgotten again."

When you adopt from Forever Home, you're not just taking a pet home. You're giving a rescued animal the security, warmth, and love they were robbed of. Every adoption creates a ripple — one life saved makes space for another to be rescued.

  • ✓ Every pet is vaccinated, dewormed & vet-cleared before adoption
  • ✓ Our team provides full guidance and post-adoption support
  • ✓ We match pets carefully with the right family for the right life
  • ✓ No hidden fees — adoption is affordable and transparent
How It Works

The Adoption Journey

Four simple, loving steps stand between you and your new best friend.

1

Browse Profiles

Explore our available animals online or visit our shelter to meet them in person. Let your heart guide you.

2

Express Interest

Fill out our simple adoption inquiry form or contact us directly. We'll respond within 24 hours with warmth.

3

Meet & Bond

Come to our shelter for a meet-and-greet session. Spend time with your chosen companion and feel the connection.

4

Take Them Home

Complete the adoption paperwork, pay the nominal fee, and welcome your new family member home forever.

Who Can Adopt

Adoption Eligibility

We want every adoption to be a permanent, happy one. Here's what we look for in a forever family.

✅

You're a Great Match If…

  • ✓ You have a stable, pet-friendly living space
  • ✓ You're committed to long-term care and companionship
  • ✓ You can provide regular food, vet visits, and attention
  • ✓ All family members agree and are excited to adopt
  • ✓ You have time, patience, and genuine love to give
🐾
⚠️

Please Reconsider If…

  • ✗ Your landlord or housing society prohibits pets
  • ✗ You're adopting impulsively or as a gift for others
  • ✗ You cannot commit to the pet's full lifespan
  • ✗ There are strong allergies in the household
  • ✗ You travel constantly with no one to care for them
🏠
What to Prepare

What You'll Need to Bring

A few simple things help us ensure every adoption is responsible, safe, and full of love.

🪪

Valid ID Proof

Any government-issued photo ID (Aadhaar, PAN, Passport) to verify your identity and address.

🏠

Residence Proof

A document showing your current address — electricity bill, rental agreement, or bank statement.

📝

Adoption Form

A short form about your lifestyle and home environment to help us find your perfect match.

📸

Home Photos

Optional but helpful — a few photos of your living space so we can ensure it's safe for your new pet.

💳

Adoption Fee

A small, nominal fee that covers vaccinations, deworming, and initial medical care for your pet.

❤️

Love & Commitment

The most important requirement — an open heart, patient hands, and a promise to never give up on them.
